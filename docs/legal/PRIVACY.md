| 권한 | 사용 목적 |

## English

### 1. Overview
Circle Camera ("the App") is a mobile camera app that lets you place a circle on the camera screen and frame a faraway subject (sun, tower, moon, person, etc.) inside it. The App is designed with privacy as a first principle. **All processing happens entirely on your device, and camera frames and photos are never transmitted to any server.**

### 2. Information We Collect and Process
- The App **requires no account** and does not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email, or phone number.
- The App uses **no analytics server, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking.**
- The camera preview (viewfinder) and any captured or edited photos are processed **on your device only.**

### 3. Device Permissions and Their Purpose
| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Display the live viewfinder and provide the circle alignment guide |
| Photo Library (add) | Save captured or edited results to your photo library |
| Photo Library (read) | Load an existing photo for the edit mode |

Frames and photos accessed through these permissions are not transmitted off the device. You can revoke permissions at any time in your operating system settings.

### 4. Data Transmission and Third-Party Sharing
The App does **not upload camera frames, photos, or any other data to external servers, and does not share or sell data to third parties.** Saved results reside in your device's photo library, and any further handling (sharing, deletion, etc.) is performed by you.

### 5. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because all processing occurs on-device, no children's data is stored in or transmitted by the App.

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion
The App does not separately retain user data on its own. Captured and edited results are saved to your photo library and can be deleted by you at any time. Removing the App also removes the temporary data it used.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy due to legal or service changes. When we do, we will revise the effective date in this document.
