### Full Description
That shot you saw in travel Reels — now take it yourself. Circle Camera puts a circle on your camera screen so you can frame a faraway sun, moon, tower, or person neatly inside it.

Move and resize the circle freely, and a real-time alignment gauge with direction arrows tells you when it's lined up just right. You press the shutter yourself — no auto-capture, just you catching the moment you want.

After shooting, switch to edit mode to load an existing photo, apply circle framing and a vignette, and export the result.

Everything happens on your device. Your photos and camera frames are never sent to any server — no account, no ad tracking.

**Key Features**
- Place and resize a circle on the screen
- Real-time alignment gauge + direction arrow guide
- You press the shutter (no auto-capture)
- Edit mode: load a photo, apply circle framing + vignette, save
- 100% on-device, no uploads, no account, no tracking
